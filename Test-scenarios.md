
LOGIN TEST SCENARIOS

TS_LOGIN_001: Verify that a user can log in with valid credentials.
TS_LOGIN_002: Verify that login fails when a valid username and incorrect password are entered.
TS_LOGIN_003: Verify that login fails when an invalid username and correct password are entered.

PRODUCT BROWSING AND SORTING SCENARIOS

TS_PRODUCT_001: Verify that available products are displayed on the Products page with their names, descriptions and prices.
TS_PRODUCT_002: Verify that products can be sorted by price from low to high and high to low.
TS_PRODUCT_003: Verify that products can be sorted alphabetically by name from A to Z and Z to A.


SHOPPING CART SCENARIOS
TS_CART_001	Verify that a user can add a product to the shopping cart.
TS_CART_002	Verify that a user can remove a product from the shopping cart.
TS_CART_003	Verify that products added to the cart remain there when navigating between the Products and Cart pages.
TS_CART_004	Verify that the shopping cart displays the products added by the user.

CHECKOUT SCENARIOS
TS_CHECKOUT_001	Verify that a user can successfully complete checkout using valid customer information.
TS_CHECKOUT_002	Verify that an appropriate validation message is displayed when required customer information is missing.
TS_CHECKOUT_003	Verify that a user can cancel the checkout process.
